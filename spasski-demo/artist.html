BIOGRAPHY

Boris Spasski

After graduating from the Russian Music Academy in Moscow, Boris Spasski was appointed permanent soloist of the Moscow Philharmonic Concert Society. The appointment led to numerous concert series and established his professional life as a soloist and chamber musician.

Performances and collaborations

He performs in Russia, Italy, Holland, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Germany, Denmark, Byelorussia (Belarus), Spain, Lithuania, Norway, Poland, Ukraine, Romania and Switzerland. His orchestral performances have included appearances with the Russian State Radio and Television Orchestra, the State Symphony Orchestra “New Russia”, the Belgorod Symphony Orchestra, the Ulyanovsk Symphony Orchestra and the Russian State Orchestra. In chamber music, he has performed with the Tchaikovsky Quartet and Dominant Quartet from Russia, the Casarati Quartet from Italy and the Aura Quartet from Switzerland.

His repertoire encompasses solo piano works and concertos from different epochs and in various musical styles, extending from Johann Sebastian Bach to the present day. Across this wide range, his playing unites virtuosity with beauty of tone, a wealth of attack and projection, natural flexibility, passion and restraint.

Education and artistic lineage

Born in Moscow and living in Switzerland for several years, Spasski belongs to a generation of musicians emerging from the so-called Moscow piano school. His musical formation took place at the Russian Music Academy in Moscow, where he studied under Professor Oleg Boschniakowitsch, an advocate of the Russian piano tradition associated with Anton Rubinstein, Sergei Rachmaninov, Konstantin Igumnov, Heinrich Neuhaus and Vladimir Sofronitsky.

This tradition places particular value on the singing quality of the piano: its ability to shape a musical line with the expressiveness, breath and subtle inflection of the human voice. In Spasski’s own playing, beauty of sound and colour meet a broad range of touch and projection, allowing musical expression to move naturally between passion and restraint.

To make the piano sing and speak, as if it were the human voice.

Festivals and international appearances

Spasski has performed at festivals in Russia and throughout Europe. His festival appearances began, among others, with the International Music Festival “Radio Orpheus presents” in Russia in 2001 and Schubertiade in Switzerland in 2003. In 2004 he appeared at an International Music Festival in Russia and the Summer Music Festival by Lake Zurich.

In 2005 he performed at the Sarajevo Winter festival in Bosnia and Herzegovina and the Vendsyssel Festival in Denmark. The following year brought appearances at an International Music Festival in Russia, Nyborg Slotskoncerter in Denmark and the 35th Langenargener Sommerkonzerte in Germany. Further invitations included the Christopher Summer Festival in Lithuania (2007), “Les Sommets du Classique” in Switzerland (2008) and the Barcelona Festival in Spain (2009).

In 2010 he appeared at Klavierissimo in Switzerland, the Winter Music International Festival in Sochi under the artistic direction of Yuri Bashmet, the 39th Langenargener Sommerkonzerte in Germany and the Engadiner Festival in Switzerland. His later appearances included an International Music Festival in Russia (2011), Quedlinburger Musiksommer in Germany (2012), Krim Festival (2013) and “Music without Borders” Festival (2014) in Ukraine, Klassik Bülach in Switzerland (2016), and in 2018 Zilele Muzicale Tîrgumureșene in Romania, the Polivanovo Festival in Russia and Brandenburgische Sommerkonzerte in Germany.

Recordings and broadcasts

Boris Spasski’s first audio CD appeared in October 2000 and was devoted to Pyotr Ilyich Tchaikovsky’s The Seasons. In December of that year, the magazine Das musikalische Leben praised the recording as “a poetic, immensely talented interpretation”. His second CD, released in July 2003, brought together the Ballades and Scherzi of Frédéric Chopin. A review in the Neue Zürcher Zeitung described him as “a pianist and musician who is worthwhile to remember”.

In November 2008 his third CD appeared, featuring Chopin’s Nocturnes, Fantaisie-Impromptu, Barcarolle and Berceuse. Alongside his disc recordings, Spasski has made recordings for the Russian State Radio, Radio Classic and Radio Orpheus, as well as for broadcasting companies in Bosnia and Herzegovina, Denmark, Byelorussia (Belarus) and Lithuania.
